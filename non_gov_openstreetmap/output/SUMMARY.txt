Acronyms & jargon (quick guide)

OSM — OpenStreetMap: crowd-sourced map/POI database.

POI — Point of Interest: venues like restaurants, bakeries, shops.

GDELT — Global Database of Events, Language & Tone: news monitoring dataset/API.

VADER — Valence Aware Dictionary and sEntiment Reasoner: sentiment model for short texts.

TF-IDF — Term Frequency–Inverse Document Frequency: text weighting for topic modeling/search.

NMF — Non-negative Matrix Factorization: topic modeling method to cluster themes.

DBSCAN — Density-Based Spatial Clustering: finds geographic clusters without pre-setting cluster counts.

NER — Named Entity Recognition: finds place names etc. in text.

Nominatim — OSM’s reverse-geocoder (coords → city/state).
